statcheck-ml

Finds reported test results with a character model. Checks each p-value with closed-form arithmetic. The model never judges; the verdict is a computation.

Runs in this tab. Nothing leaves your machine. Labels behind the model are bronze (three rater agents, no human gold set); read the numbers with that in mind.

statcheck-ml stands on statcheck, by Michèle B. Nuijten and Sacha Epskamp, with Willem Sleegers, Edoardo Costantini, Paul van der Laken, Sean Rife, John Sakaluk, Chris Hartgerink and Steve Haroz. Michèle Nuijten, the mother of statcheck, showed that a machine can read the statistics a paper reports and catch the ones that do not add up. This page only teaches that idea to read a damaged PDF.
