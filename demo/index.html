statcheck-ml

Finds reported test results with a character model. Checks each p-value with closed-form arithmetic. The model never judges; the verdict is a computation.

Runs in this tab. Nothing leaves your machine. Labels behind the model are bronze (three rater agents, with human coders as a fourth vote on part of the data); read the numbers with that in mind.
